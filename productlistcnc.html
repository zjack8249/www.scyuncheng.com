CNG/LPG Vehicle Conversion Systems

CNG/LPG vehicle conversion systems enable compatible petrol vehicles to operate with compressed natural gas or liquefied petroleum gas. A complete system combines fuel control, pressure regulation, gas injection and safety components selected for the vehicle engine and local market requirements.

What Is Included in a CNG/LPG Conversion System?

A CNG/LPG conversion system typically includes a pressure reducer, injector rail, gas ECU, wiring harness, sensors, solenoid valves, fuel changeover switch, hoses and installation accessories. The final configuration depends on fuel type, engine cylinder count, injection type and vehicle application.
