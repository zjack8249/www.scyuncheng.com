Why Choose Us

SCYUNCHENG supports CNG/LPG conversion projects for distributors, conversion workshops, fleet applications and OEM customers. Our product range includes conversion kits, reducers, injector rails, ECU kits, wiring harnesses, valves, hoses, filters and related automotive gas components. Customers can discuss product-category selection, component matching, OEM requirements and export project needs according to the vehicle application, required gas fuel and target market. Portable EV charging equipment is developed as a separate new product line.
